ONE MORE Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Data collected by the developer

ONE MORE does not send personal information, gameplay, analytics or advertising data to the developer. The app has no developer account system, advertising SDK or developer server.

Information on your device

Scores, progress, earned stickers, settings and unfinished Game Center submissions are stored locally on your device. Removing the app may remove this local information.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple Game Center, Apple processes your Game Center identity, leaderboard scores and achievements. ONE MORE sends the relevant scores and achievement progress to Game Center. Apple handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

The game is sold as a one-time App Store purchase. Payments are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your full payment-card details.

Children

ONE MORE is a general-audience casual game and is not specifically directed at children.

Contact

For privacy or support enquiries, visit the ONE MORE support page.
