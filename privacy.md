# CaseReady Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 21 September 2026**

CaseReady is designed to help users in England understand and manage consumer-rights issues relating to cars and motorcycles.

## Data we collect

CaseReady does **not collect personal data from you**.

The app does not require an account, does not use advertising or tracking, and does not send your case information to a CaseReady server.

## Information stored on your device

Case information you enter, including questionnaire answers, manual facts, complaint details and uploaded evidence, is stored locally on your device.

This may include documents, photographs, scans and other files that you choose to add to a case.

You remain in control of this information and can delete local case data from the app.

## Purchases

CaseReady offers an optional one-time in-app purchase.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. CaseReady does not receive your full payment-card details.

Apple may process purchase and account information in accordance with Apple's own privacy policies.

## Camera, Photos and Files

If you choose to use these features, CaseReady may request permission to access:

- the camera, to photograph or scan evidence;
- Photos, to select evidence images;
- Files, to import documents or other evidence.

These permissions are used only to provide the feature you request.

## Artificial intelligence

The current public version of CaseReady does not use production AI to analyse your documents or automatically determine legal outcomes.

Legal assessments are produced using deterministic app rules based on the information you provide and confirm.

## Third-party content and links

CaseReady may display or link to official third-party information, such as legislation or regulatory guidance.

If you follow an external link, the privacy practices of that external website will apply.

## Data sharing

CaseReady does not sell, rent or share your case data with advertisers or data brokers.

If you choose to share a complaint, case pack or other document using iOS sharing features, the information is sent only to the destination you select.

## Data retention

Case data remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or otherwise clear the app's local storage.

## Children

CaseReady is not specifically directed at children.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised date.

## Contact

For privacy or support enquiries relating to CaseReady, please use the support contact details published on the CaseReady support page.
