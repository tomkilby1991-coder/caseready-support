CaseReady Support

Help with using CaseReady on iPhone.

Need help?

If you are having a problem with CaseReady, have found a bug, or need help using a feature, email the developer directly.

Email Support

Support email: tom.kilby1991@outlook.com

When contacting support

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, what you were trying to do, and what happened. Screenshots can also be helpful.

Privacy

Do not email sensitive financial information, passwords or payment-card details. CaseReady case data is stored locally on your device.

CaseReady currently supports vehicle consumer-rights cases in England for cars and motorcycles.
